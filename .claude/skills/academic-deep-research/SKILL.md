# Academic Deep Research

## Workflow
1. Start from an existing literature pool or run `literature-search` first.

2. Build an evidence matrix across papers:
- research question
- methods and assumptions
- datasets or settings
- metrics and results
- limitations

3. Synthesize at three levels:
- Level 1: what is known (high-confidence consensus)
- Level 2: what is contested (methodological or empirical conflict)
- Level 3: what is missing (high-value future directions)

4. Produce decision-oriented outputs:
- opportunity map for new work
- methodological recommendations
- prioritized experiment ideas

5. Attach traceability:
- map each major claim to supporting paper IDs
- mark low-confidence inferences explicitly

## Guardrails
- Distinguish direct evidence from interpretation.
- Do not over-generalize from small or narrow samples.
- Surface contradictory evidence instead of suppressing it.
