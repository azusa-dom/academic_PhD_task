# Author actions before any JCMR submission

The scientific and production revision is complete at machine/AI-assisted level. The package is not submission-approved until the corresponding author completes the items below.

## Required scientific review

- Read the full main manuscript, Supplementary Tables S1–S2 and the atomic claim/source ledger; confirm all interpretations and numeric statements.
- Confirm that “not reported”, “not inspected” and “not directly evaluated” are used as intended and do not conflict with sources known to the author.
- Decide whether the bounded conclusion adequately represents the intended contribution: strongest support for specified circumferential endpoints, weaker evidence for radial and focal extent/timing claims.
- Confirm that no prior Stanford/STRAUS exploratory output is being relied upon as validation. The current manuscript treats those resources prospectively and does not report a new experiment.

## Authorship and declarations

- Confirm the final author list, order, affiliations and CRediT contributions. The package does not infer a supervisor or contributor.
- Supply the full postal correspondence address required by the current JCMR checklist.
- Supply/approve the competing-interests, funding and acknowledgement statements; obtain permission from anyone named in acknowledgements.
- Confirm whether consent-for-publication wording should remain “not applicable”.
- Review and approve the AI-use disclosure against institutional and current publisher policy.

## Figures and rights

- Inspect every retained main, supplementary and graphical-abstract figure for scientific accuracy, rights and accessibility. v9 preserved the selected v8.1 assets byte-for-byte and did not alter any image, Draw.io source, text or layout.
- Confirm that the inherited graphical abstract satisfies the live upload dimensions/format and current AI/illustration policy. If editorial clarification or manual reconstruction is required, that is an author/editor decision outside v9.
- Confirm alt text and figure captions. Any future figure change will invalidate the v9 figure-hash manifest and requires a new build/QA pass.

## Journal route and final submission checks

- Confirm whether JCMR invited the review or requires a proposal/presubmission enquiry; no editor contact has been made.
- Check the live JCMR author page and submission portal for changed requirements, especially article type, graphical abstract, Plain English Summary, declarations and file naming.
- Replace draft wording in the cover letter/presubmission enquiry only after confirming the route. Do not claim invitation, endorsement or prior contact unless documented.
- Approve the final title, abstract, keywords and Plain English Summary.
- Perform the final portal preview and author proof. This package does not log in, upload, email, sign declarations or submit.
