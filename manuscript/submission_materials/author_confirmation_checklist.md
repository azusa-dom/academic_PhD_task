# Author confirmation checklist

Nothing in this checklist is marked as approved on the author's behalf.

## Authorship and identity

- [ ] Confirm final author list, order and spelling.
- [ ] Confirm that each author meets the journal's authorship criteria and approves submission.
- [ ] Confirm the University of Manchester affiliation wording.
- [ ] Supply and confirm the full postal correspondence address.
- [ ] Confirm email and ORCID.
- [ ] Obtain permission before naming anyone in acknowledgements.

## Scientific content

- [ ] Read the manuscript, supplement, all figure text and all captions in full.
- [ ] Verify every quantitative statement against the cited source and evidence ledger.
- [ ] Confirm the narrative-review scope and the stated search limitations.
- [ ] Confirm that the proposed title and conclusions match the intended claims.
- [ ] Confirm any intended clinical recommendations with appropriately qualified co-authors, if applicable.

## Declarations

- [ ] Confirm funding and grant numbers, or an explicit no-funding statement.
- [ ] Confirm competing interests for every author.
- [ ] Confirm author contributions using the journal's requested taxonomy.
- [ ] Confirm ethics/consent statements remain “not applicable” for this review.
- [ ] Confirm data/materials wording.
- [ ] Approve the AI-assistance declaration and adapt it to the live submission field if needed.
- [ ] Confirm originality, exclusive consideration and absence of duplicate publication before signing the cover letter.

## Figures and rights

- [ ] Inspect the native replacements in Figures 1, 2, 3, S2 and S3 and confirm that their scientific meaning is correct.
- [ ] Review the native-source audit showing zero remaining embedded image styles; independently confirm that the final artwork may be submitted and that no further permission statement is required.
- [ ] Review the figure provenance/rights log and alt text.
- [ ] Approve the graphical abstract at approximately 500 × 200 pixels.
- [ ] Confirm the live Elsevier/JCMR interpretation of Codex-assisted deterministic graphical-abstract construction; obtain editorial confirmation or manually reconstruct it if required.
