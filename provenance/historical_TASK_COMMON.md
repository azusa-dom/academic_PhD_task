## How to read and assess
Tier 1: retrieve full relevant Methods, Results, figures/tables/captions and Discussion limitations. Read every specified section, not just search snippets. Priority claims require a section-aware reading note documenting what was read, method/estimand, population, split, reference, key results, restrictions and exact source location. Do not call a paper fully read if only selected sections were read. Tier 1.5: abstract plus relevant reconstruction/strain results tables and methods needed to interpret participants, exclusions and comparators. Tier 2: abstract-level confirmation is sufficient ONLY if it directly supports the narrow manuscript claim; escalate to full methods/results if numbers, supervision, data split, strain definition, reference nature or inferential scope cannot be supported by the abstract. Tier 3: verify the actual definitional/resource claim from official primary records; escalate numeric/measurement claims (especially CMAC landmarks/0.84 mm). Do not accept the checklist's assertion that reviewers will not question these claims.

For all source claims distinguish: bibliographic identity; factual/numeric fidelity including units and denominators; support for the sentence; correct comparator and measurement definition/timepoint/scale; validation level and generalization; consistency across manuscript/supplement/figures. Distinguish cohort/subject/slice/segment/examination counts; end-systolic vs peak; Ecc/Err/Ell; endocardial vs transmural; engineering vs Green–Lagrange strain; correlation vs ICC/agreement/equivalence; SD vs CI vs LoA; percent vs percentage points; internal/temporal/external evaluation; simulated known deformation vs human reference agreement; learned segmentation/contour displacement vs true material correspondence.

For numeric or source inconsistencies, inspect the original table/figure pixels when accessible. Do not “correct” mutually inconsistent source Results and tables by guessing. Record both source locations/values and the exact supported manuscript handling. Negative claims such as “no external validation”, “no strain evaluation”, “no intensity loss” or “not reported” require reading sufficient relevant Methods/Results/supplement/limitations; abstract absence is not evidence of absence. Distinguish not reported, not found in accessed sections, and directly contradicted. If evidence is insufficient, use NOT_VERIFIED, not INCORRECT. Don't classify a defensible author inference as an established source result; label and assess the inference separately.

Retrieve via public publisher/PMC/PubMed/author institutional repositories, official conference proceedings/arXiv or official datasets. Search only public citations/DOIs/titles; never send unpublished paragraphs to search. Record DOI, URL, version, retrieval date, file/hash, access depth and sections actually read. Use public alternatives for inaccessible full text, with a bounded attempt log. No access-control circumvention, raw patient data or new experiments. Tools may retrieve public full texts and render existing article figures locally; no image generation. Text-only extraction cannot verify figure-only numerals. If primary pages are inaccessible but the existing PDF is complete and bibliographic identity is confirmed, it can support a source-specific audit with clear provenance.
