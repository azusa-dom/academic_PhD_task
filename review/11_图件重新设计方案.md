## Figure 1 — 测量链与三道可分离的失败点

**主张**:区域应变不是"从图像读出来"的,而是五步链的输出;链上三个失败点(表示容量、估计过程、输入信息)可以分别检验。

**对应原文**
- §2.1 第 1 段(body.tex 27 行):"The image therefore supplies anatomical and intensity cues, not a persistent identity label for every tissue element."
- §2.1 式(1)(2)(36–47 行)及其后一句:"The equations do not guarantee that φ is correct. They state how a chosen mapping is converted into a chosen strain."
- §3.1 第 2–3 段(120–124 行):"Two statements about priors are easy to conflate… The first concerns what a representation can express… The second concerns the estimation process… A third factor, the information actually present in the input images, is separate again." 以及归因实验三步。
- §5.3 三个问题(293–297 行)。
- 摘要第 2 段"common causal chain: image formation and sampling; segmentation or correspondence estimation; regularisation and representation; strain calculation and aggregation; and validation."

**面板与文案**
- **a 链条(横向五个节点,左→右)**
  1. `Image formation & sampling` 下注:bSSFP cine · spatial/temporal resolution · reconstruction
  2. `Correspondence estimation` 下注:contours or dense field · estimator + prior
  3. `Representation & regularisation` 下注:voxel / spline / Fourier / FE / learned
  4. `Strain definition & aggregation` 下注:E = ½(FᵀF − I) · axes · layer · reference · peak rule
  5. `Validation target` 下注:known motion · DENSE/tagging · scan–rescan · LGE · outcome
  节点 1 灰,2–4 青,5 橙。箭头为"输入→输出"(实线)。
- **b 三道失败点(在 a 下方,三个向上指的标注框)**
  - 指向节点 3:`Representation capacity — can the basis express the lesion?` 检验方法注:`project the known field onto the function space`
  - 指向节点 2:`Estimation — does the optimiser/network recover it?` 检验方法注:`vary α (penalty weight) with images fixed`
  - 指向节点 1:`Input sufficiency — do the images contain the cue?` 检验方法注:`re-image the same motion at finer resolution / more views`
  三框用虚线连接到对应节点,标注 `separately testable`。
- **c 一句式结论条(底部)**:`Validation must follow the finest claimed spatial and temporal scale, not inherit credibility from an upstream task.`(原文 §2.2 末句,60 行)

**图注草稿**:From cine images to a regional strain report and where the chain can fail. (a) Five links separate what routine cine observes (grey) from what an estimator infers (teal) and what a validation target can establish (orange). (b) Three failure points map onto distinct links and can be tested separately: representation capacity by projecting a known field onto the estimator's function space, estimation by varying the regularisation weight with images fixed, and input sufficiency by re-imaging the same motion. (c) Validation should follow the finest claimed scale. Conceptual schematic; no study data.

## Figure 2 — 同一轮廓,不同材料对应(解析反例)

**主张**:mask 完全一致(Dice = 1,Hausdorff = 0)的两种映射可以有不同的周向拉伸;轮廓一致不能证明材料对应。

**对应原文**
- §2.2 第 1 段(51 行):"Both mappings have Dice overlap of one and Hausdorff distance of zero for the masks, yet their circumferential stretch varies differently with position."
- §2.2 末段(60 行):"Chamber volume can be estimated from boundaries… Transmural or voxelwise strain additionally requires intramyocardial correspondence and spatial derivatives."
- §3.1(122 行):"many fields achieve nearly the same data term while implying different displacement gradients, and the prior selects among them".

**面板与文案**(**全部为解析构造,不用任何真实图像**;v9 Figure 2 的面板 a 用了真实 cine/tagging/DENSE 图,这里改为示意)
- **a 三种采集提供的信息(三个并排小环,示意)**:`Routine cine: boundaries + weak texture`(灰环,内部浅纹理);`Tagging: prepared grid`(灰环 + 橙色网格线);`DENSE: phase-encoded displacement`(灰环 + 橙色小箭头场)。下注:`only tagging/DENSE carry material labels`。
- **b 两种对应(两个同样的环,ED→ES)**:映射 1 `θ' = θ`(八个标记点等角分布,青色);映射 2 `θ' = θ + a sin θ, a = 0.5`(同八个点被重新分布,青色);两环的内外轮廓完全相同(灰)。中间标注 `identical masks: Dice = 1, Hausdorff = 0`。
- **c 周向拉伸曲线**:横轴 θ(0–2π),纵轴 λ_θ;映射 1 为常数 1(虚线);映射 2 为 `λθ = 1 + a cos θ`,范围 0.5–1.5(实线青)。标注 `orientation preserved for |a| < 1`。
- 角标:`analytic example — not a measured cardiac result; stretch ratio is not Green–Lagrange strain`。

**图注草稿**:Identical contours do not determine the same material correspondence. (a) Schematic of the information each acquisition supplies: routine cine provides boundaries and weak texture, tagging a prepared pattern, DENSE phase-encoded displacement. (b) Two material mappings between the same end-diastolic and end-systolic annular contours: angular identity, and θ′ = θ + a sin θ with a = 0.5. Both give identical masks (Dice = 1, Hausdorff = 0). (c) Their circumferential stretch λθ = 1 + a cos θ differs, ranging 0.5–1.5. This is an analytic counterexample, not a measurement of any estimator's error.

**Alt text**:Three schematic annuli showing cine, tagging and DENSE information; two annuli with identical outlines but differently distributed marker points; a plot of circumferential stretch versus angle contrasting a flat line with a cosine.
