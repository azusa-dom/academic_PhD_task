## 你定下的两条规则怎么落实

**规则一:禁止"不是…而是…"类句式。** 机器扫描了四类模式:`not … but …`、`rather than`、`not only … but also`、`instead of`,以及作为句子连接符的破折号 `---`,和 `Conversely / Likewise / Similarly / Equally / importantly` 这类假连接词。改前主文 45 处 + 21 处破折号 + 10 处假连接词,摘要 4 处,补充 4 处;改后全部为 0。处理方式有三种:把对比改写成一个正面陈述("The equations do not guarantee … They state how …" → "These equations state how a chosen mapping is converted into a chosen strain; they say nothing about whether φ is correct");把"X, not Y"拆成两句、各带自己的理由;或者在必须保留科学对比时用分号和显式理由("it supports …, and it warns against …; it does not justify …")。两个小节标题也改了:"Repeatability establishes precision, not accuracy" → "…, and leaves accuracy open";"Tissue references test biological correspondence, not motion truth" → "…, and leave motion truth open";"Reconstruction evidence is relevant but endpoint-specific" → "… relevant and endpoint-specific"。

**规则二:句与句之间必须有逻辑关系,不凭空换话题。** 按 Logic First 逐句检查"第二句如何推进第一句"。主要改法是把隐含的关系写出来:(a) 用回指开头承接上一句的对象("That compression has real value … The same compression also hides information");(b) 给每段一个明确的主题句,并让段末句接到下一段("The appropriate response is to pair simulation with material-sensitive and clinical tests, which the next subsections take up");(c) 把原来并列堆叠的事实改成有先后的推进("Chamber volume can be estimated from boundaries alone. A global contour-length change is also … A segmental average additionally requires … Transmural or voxelwise strain requires, on top of that, …");(d) 删掉或改写凭空出现的句子——例如 §2.1 原来"The distinction in Fig. 1 is causal, not semantic"后面直接跳到公式,现改为"The separation … is a causal one, and it can be written down",公式成为上一句的展开;§5 引言原来两句并列,现在第二句说明"下面四个小节分别处理哪类矛盾"。
