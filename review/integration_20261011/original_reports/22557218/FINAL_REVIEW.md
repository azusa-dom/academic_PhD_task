# Final adversarial review

## Decision

**Status: AUTHOR_REVIEW_REQUIRED.** The revision is scientifically and technically coherent enough for accountable author review. It is not accepted, submitted or certified journal-ready. All 18 defects in the issue register are closed as `resolved` or `resolved_by_narrowing`; remaining items below are evidence- or author-process limitations rather than concealed manuscript claims.

## 1. Contribution of the synthesis

The review now makes a distinct and useful contribution: it connects observable cine cues, inferred correspondence, strain definition and validation target to the four attributes of a regional abnormality—magnitude, location, extent and timing. The contribution is a critical synthesis and proposed evaluation logic, not a new estimator or systematic evidence grade. The method chronology supports this argument without dominating it, while Supplementary Table S3 preserves breadth.

Adversarial finding: the framework could still be mistaken for a consensus standard if read selectively. The section title, opening paragraph, abstract and conclusion now repeatedly label the programme as proposed author synthesis, which is the minimum defensible remedy.

## 2. Clarity and organisation

The final funnel is coherent: clinical problem → image information → inferred mapping → estimand → method assumptions → validation evidence → reconciliation → proposed programme → practical reporting. Technical definitions precede comparisons, and the validation section is now the evidential centre. Repetition was removed mainly from the estimator catalogue rather than by deleting clinical or quantitative context.

Adversarial finding: Tables 2–4 and S1–S3 are information-dense. They are legible in the author-review PDF but should not be treated as final journal typesetting. Figure 3 also requires careful author inspection at intended size.

## 3. Strength of cited evidence

Central numerical claims were checked against accessible primary full text and corrected at endpoint level: MRXCAT2.0, StrainNet, Jahromi 2026, Wehner, Cao, Militaru, Kihlberg, Brandt, Strain-8 and REGAIN. Gao was limited to primary-abstract claims. The manuscript no longer infers significance/equivalence from overlapping standard deviations, no longer treats MRXCAT slice means as scar-region accuracy, and reports both the Jahromi TOST result and paired difference.

Adversarial finding: this is not full-text verification of all 94 cited records. The inherited ledger covers 69 keys and points to absent local source files; several lineage and reconstruction records remain at abstract or metadata depth. The wording is now limited accordingly, but accountable human source verification remains necessary.

## 4. Coverage and comparability of validation

The revised paper correctly keeps mask overlap, displacement error, strain agreement, repeatability, tissue discrimination and prognosis as different endpoints. Participant, slice, segment and pixel analyses are not treated as interchangeable. Positive evidence is retained for bounded whole-slice/global/segmental circumferential estimands, and negative or inconsistent radial/regional results remain product-, definition- and setting-specific.

Adversarial finding: the search is capped, single-screen and not systematic. Therefore “only”, “rare” and “not identified” statements are explicitly tied to the recorded search. Direct scar-level magnitude, boundary, extent and focal timing validation remains an evidence gap, not evidence that cine algorithms fail.

## 5. Soundness of reasoning and recommendations

The distinction among representation capacity, estimation error and input sufficiency is logically useful and prevents causal overreach. The same-contour construction establishes only its mathematical proposition. Synthetic results are not presented as patient-level diagnostic utility; LGE and outcomes are not presented as motion truth; repeatability is not validity. Recommendations follow the intended claim rather than imposing every endpoint on every study.

Adversarial finding: no experiment in this review validates the complete proposed programme or isolates all three causal sources. The programme is therefore a justified research proposal, not an established protocol. Clinical use still requires method-, acquisition-, population-, scale-, reference- and decision-specific evidence.
