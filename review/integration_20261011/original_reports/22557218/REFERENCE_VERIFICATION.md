## Material corrections

- MRXCAT2.0 Figure 14 averages three mid-ventricular short-axis slices. Its error values are not scar-region errors. The reported means and SDs do not constitute a between-case test.
- The 2026 cine–DENSE study used a selected ±3.5 percentage-point equivalence margin. Its TOST conclusion coexists with a −2.7-point mean difference, `p<0.001` and `d=-0.89`; the revised manuscript reports both.
- StrainNet’s displacement EPE used DENSE-magnitude contours. The ordinary-cine comparison was a separate Ecc analysis in 59 test participants. These endpoints remain separate.
- Kihlberg’s AUC values concern discrimination of segments with >50% transmural LGE, not a ranking of displacement accuracy.
- Repeatability evidence is labelled precision, not validity.
