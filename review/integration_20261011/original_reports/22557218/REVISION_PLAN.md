# Revision plan

## Narrative thesis

Routine cine CMR contains useful anatomical and temporal cues but does not directly label material points. Regional motion/strain claims are therefore conditional on acquisition, estimator, estimand and validation target. The review's contribution is to show which claim each validation design can support and to identify the finer attributes—magnitude, location, extent and timing—that remain insufficiently tested.
