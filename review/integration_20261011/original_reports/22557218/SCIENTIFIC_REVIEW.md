# Scientific review of the supplied v10 baseline

## Overall assessment

The manuscript asks a valuable and unusually precise question: for a stated cine acquisition, estimator and measurement definition, which attributes of regional dysfunction—magnitude, location, extent and timing—are actually supported, at what scale and against which reference? Its strongest contribution is not a new estimator but a critical synthesis that separates observed cine cues, inferred correspondence, strain definition and validation target. The analytic same-contour counterexample and the validation-target hierarchy make that distinction concrete.

The paper is already technically rich and generally cautious. Its main revision need is calibration rather than wholesale replacement. Several central statements are stronger than the accessible audit trail warrants; the MRXCAT2.0 example mixes prescribed scar-region strain with whole-slice estimator errors; method-family coverage occupies more space than the primary question requires; and proposals for future evaluation occasionally read like conclusions about current capability. The revised paper should retain the positive evidence—especially matched circumferential-strain results and StrainNet’s segmental comparison—while tying each inference to its exact estimand, analysis unit and reference.

## Contribution of the synthesis

The four-attribute framing is useful because segmental agreement is not the same endpoint as lesion localisation or extent. The manuscript also correctly distinguishes analytical validity, biological association and clinical utility. These ideas are individually established, but their joint application to regional cine-CMR motion/strain validation is a defensible narrative-review contribution. It should be presented as an organising synthesis, not as a validated regulatory framework or a claim of exhaustive novelty.

## Principal scientific risks

1. **Auditability of review methods.** The supplied ledger contains 135 claim–source pairs but only 69 unique citekeys, whereas 94 records are actively cited. It labels 75 pairs “full_text” and points to 32 unique `sources/...` paths, none of which is bundled. This does not prove the historical readings did not occur, but it prevents this revision from independently reproducing those depth claims. The manuscript should say exactly what the supplied records support and distinguish the fresh targeted checks from the historical search.
2. **MRXCAT2.0 inference.** Ground-truth scar/remote strains are region-specific, whereas the DeepStrain errors are averages of three mid-ventricular short-axis slices. The latter cannot show recovery of scar magnitude, boundary or extent. No between-case inferential comparison was reported; overlap of descriptive SDs is not a significance or equivalence test. The author-reported “lowest performance” can be attributed to the source, but it should not be converted into a statistically established ranking.
3. **Cine–DENSE equivalence.** The 2026 study found statistical equivalence for endocardial circumferential strain within a selected ±3.5 percentage-point margin, while also reporting a mean difference of −2.7 percentage points, a significant paired difference and a large effect size. Both findings are needed. The analysis used two slices per participant, which should not be casually described as 80 independent observations.
4. **Coverage language.** “The one independent test” and “remains untested” are only defensible within the recorded, capped search. The wording should remain explicitly bounded and should not imply field-wide exhaustiveness.
5. **Comparability.** Some tables place ICCs, bias, AUC, repeatability coefficients and known-motion errors near one another. The text states that they are not pooled, but the visual and prose should repeatedly preserve their distinct targets and units.
6. **Proposal/result boundary.** Projection tests, controlled regularisation sweeps and the three-stage programme are recommendations derived from the synthesis. They are not validated findings of the review.

## Structure and writing

The opening funnel works: clinical need → regional question → observable/inferred distinction → validation. The middle is overlong because method chronology and method-family description partly duplicate Supplementary Table S3. The main text should keep method families only to explain the assumptions that matter for regional inference, moving catalogue-like detail to the supplement. Validation evidence should become the narrative centre. Each paragraph should begin with a claim about what an evidence type can or cannot establish, followed by representative studies and the support boundary.

The prose is mostly controlled but sometimes uses abstract nouns and repeated cautionary formulations. The revision should shorten repeated “does not establish” clauses by stating the positive supported endpoint first and the residual limitation once. British spelling and established terminology should be retained.

## Figures, tables and PDF

The figures are scientifically purposeful and preserve a consistent white/sage/olive/terracotta design. At the baseline manuscript’s approximately 160-mm text width, artwork designed at 190 mm is scaled to about 84.2%, reducing nominal 7.06-pt labels to about 5.94 pt. Figure 3 is especially dense. The manuscript source should place figures at their intended readable size within the actual A4 layout or regenerate them for the placement width; simply claiming compliance from the native file is insufficient. The current full-page placements are otherwise unclipped in the baseline contact-sheet inspection.

Figure 4 should use one evidential sentence everywhere: prescribed scar-region strain was reduced relative to remote tissue, while estimator errors were reported only as slice averages; no scar-region recovery error or between-case inferential test was reported. Tables should retain denominator and unit columns, which are among the manuscript’s strengths.

## Minimum publishable revision

- Replace unbounded uniqueness and absence claims with search-bounded wording.
- Correct the MRXCAT2.0 statistical interpretation in abstract, main text, Figure 4 caption and tables.
- Report the 2026 equivalence finding with its selected margin, mean difference and scope.
- Make the historical/fresh verification distinction explicit and provide a reproducible verification table.
- Reduce catalogue-like method detail without deleting substantive family coverage.
- Rebuild figures at readable placement size and update stale compliance/provenance counts.
- Preserve author-confirmation flags and label the package `AUTHOR_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
